All FAQs
Are pets okay during a cleaning?

Yes. We prefer pets in a crate or a closed room while we work, but friendly pets are fine. We work it out with each client at the walk-through.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

We provide all supplies and equipment unless you ask us not to. Pet- and family-friendly products are available; we agree on what to use during the walk-through.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us 24 hours' notice. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice incur a 30% fee.

Should I tip the cleaners?

Tips are never required. If you'd like to leave one, please give cash directly to the cleaners.

What happens if something gets damaged?

If something is damaged during a cleaning, we cover it. Larger items are covered by our insurance.

What languages do you speak, and what are your hours?

English, Portuguese and Spanish. Residential service runs 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM; commercial cleaning is available 24/7.
