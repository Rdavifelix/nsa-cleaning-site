Good to know

Commercial cleaning questions

What business owners and office managers in the Albany area ask us before signing a contract.

All FAQs
Can you clean outside our business hours?

Yes. Commercial service is available 24/7. Most offices, clinics and restaurants have us come in at night, early in the morning or on weekends so your team and your customers never see a cleaning in progress.

Do we need a contract, or can we book a one-time cleaning?

Both work. Recurring contracts (nightly, weekly, bi-weekly or monthly) get a fixed schedule and a set price. One-time cleanings before an opening, after an event or ahead of an inspection are quoted the same way, after a walk-through.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. We provide all cleaning supplies and equipment unless you prefer that we use your products. If your facility requires a specific disinfectant or protocol, we agree on it during the walk-through and write it into the scope.

Are you insured?

Yes, NSA Cleaning LLC is insured. If something is damaged during a cleaning, we cover it; larger items are covered by our insurance. Our staff wear gray NSA uniforms and are trained to NSA standards.

Which areas do you serve for commercial cleaning?

We are based in Watervliet and travel up to about one hour: Albany, Troy, Schenectady, Latham, Colonie, Clifton Park, Saratoga Springs, Glens Falls, Queensbury, Lake George and the towns in between. Travel is included in your price. See the full service area.
