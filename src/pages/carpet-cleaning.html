How it works

Four steps, then time to dry

Most rooms are dry enough to walk on in socks the same day. Thick pile, humid Capital Region summers and basement rooms take longer.

  1. 1

    Walk-through

    We look at the carpet, note stains and traffic areas, and confirm the products you want us to use.

  2. 2

    Pre-treat and vacuum

    Dry soil comes out first. Spots and traffic lanes get a pre-spray so the shampoo has less to fight.

  3. 3

    Shampoo and extract

    Cleaning solution goes in, dirty water comes out. Extra passes on the worst areas.

  4. 4

    Dry and inspect

    We open windows or run fans if you allow it, and walk the rooms with you before we go.

All FAQs
How long does carpet take to dry?

Usually the same day. Thick carpet, basements and humid days can push it to overnight. Fans, open windows or the AC speed it up, and you can walk on it in clean socks meanwhile.

Can you get out pet stains and odors?

Most of them, yes. We spot-treat the stain and treat the odor at the source. Old accidents that soaked into the padding may lighten rather than disappear. We'll tell you straight at the walk-through.

Do I need to move furniture first?

Please clear small items and anything breakable. We move light furniture like chairs and side tables and clean around heavy pieces such as beds and dressers.

Do you clean sofas and other upholstery?

Yes. Sofas, sectionals, armchairs and fabric dining chairs can be cleaned on the same visit. We check the care tag first and use lower moisture so cushions dry quickly.
