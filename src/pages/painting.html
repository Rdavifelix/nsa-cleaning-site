What we paint

Interior painting done properly, from prep to cleanup

A good paint job is mostly preparation. We protect what stays, fix what we can before the first coat, and pack up only when the room is clean.

  • Walls and ceilings

    Bedrooms, kitchens, hallways, stairwells and basements. Cut in by hand at the ceiling line and around windows, rolled for an even finish.

  • Trim, doors and molding

    Baseboards, casings, picture-frame molding and interior doors. We sand lightly, fill nail holes and caulk gaps so the trim reads crisp.

  • Prep and masking

    Furniture is moved or covered, floors get drop cloths, and cabinets, counters and fixtures are masked with plastic and painter's tape. Holes and cracks are patched first.

  • Commercial repaints

    Offices, clinics, restaurants, gyms and common areas. Our commercial team is available 24/7, so we paint evenings, overnight or weekends and your business stays open.

  • Color help

    We talk through colors and sheen for each room at the walk-through and can test a patch before you commit.

  • Post-painting cleanup by our cleaning crew

    When the paint is dry we remove the masking, vacuum dust, wipe surfaces and floors, and put the furniture back. You get a finished room, not a job site.

Painting questions

All FAQs
Do I supply the paint or do you?

Either. We bring paint and all supplies by default and put the brand and color on the quote. If you already bought paint or want a specific product, we're happy to use it. Mention it at the walk-through.

How long does a room take?

It depends on the size of the room, the condition of the walls and how many coats the color needs. Trim, repairs and dark-to-light changes add time. We give you a schedule with the quote.

Can you paint my office without closing it?

Yes. Our commercial team is available 24/7, so we paint evenings, overnight or on weekends and have the space clean and back in use before your staff arrive.

What happens if something gets damaged?

We cover it. Furniture and floors are protected before we start, and if something is damaged anyway we make it right. Larger items are covered by our insurance.
