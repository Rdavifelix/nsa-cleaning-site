Good to know

Residential cleaning questions

The things clients in Albany, Troy and Saratoga Springs ask before their first visit.

All FAQs
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients give us a key, a garage code or a lockbox after the walk-through. If you would rather be home, that's fine too — we work around you.

Are pets okay during a cleaning?

Yes. We prefer pets in a crate or a closed room while we work, but friendly pets are fine — we work it out with each client. Pet-friendly products can be agreed at the walk-through.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. We provide all supplies and equipment unless you ask us to use yours — for example a specific product you prefer for hardwood floors or stone counters.

What if something gets damaged?

If we damage something, we cover it. Larger items are covered by our insurance. Tell us right away and we'll sort it out.
