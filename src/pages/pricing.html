What affects the price

What moves a cleaning quote up or down

These are the things we weigh when we price a recurring plan.

Size and layout

Bedrooms, bathrooms and square footage set the base. Extra floors, finished basements and large kitchens add time.

Current condition

A home that has not been cleaned in a while usually needs a first deep clean, then settles into the regular recurring rate.

Frequency

Weekly and bi-weekly visits stay on top of build-up, so each visit takes less time than a once-a-month reset.

Add-ons

Inside the oven or fridge, interior windows, laundry or a garage sweep. Tell us at the walk-through and we price them in.

Pets

Pet hair adds vacuuming time; pet-friendly supplies on request.

Travel

Included for any address within about an hour of Watervliet.
