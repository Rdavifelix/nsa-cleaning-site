Who books it

One cleaning, four different reasons to book it

We work with renters and owners across the Capital Region: apartments in Albany and Troy, houses in Colonie, Delmar and Clifton Park, and second homes up in Saratoga Springs and Lake George.

  • Tenants moving out

    Most leases require the unit to be returned clean. A move-out clean gives you the best shot at a full deposit refund, and you don't spend your last day in the apartment scrubbing an oven.

  • Landlords and property managers

    Turn a unit around between tenants without sending your own staff. We clean to a consistent standard on every unit, work from a lockbox, and can pair the cleaning with paint touch-ups to cut days off your vacancy.

  • Home sellers

    Before the photos, the open house or the final walk-through, an empty house needs to look cared for. Clean windowsills, spotless bathrooms and floors without a film make a real difference in how the home shows.

  • Buyers and new tenants moving in

    You don't know how the previous people lived. A move-in clean before the boxes arrive means cabinets, drawers, closets and appliances are clean before you put anything inside them.

Empty-home checklist

What a move-out clean covers, room by room

With the furniture gone we get behind the fridge, inside every cabinet and into the corner of every closet. This is the standard list; tell us if your lease or listing agent asks for anything extra.

Kitchen

  • Inside and outside of all cabinets and drawers, shelves wiped
  • Inside the oven, fridge, freezer, microwave and dishwasher
  • Stovetop, range hood, backsplash and counters degreased
  • Sink and faucet descaled, floor mopped, behind appliances where they can be moved

Bathrooms

  • Tub, shower, tile and grout scrubbed, soap scum and mildew removed
  • Toilet inside and out, base and behind
  • Vanity inside and out, mirror, fixtures, exhaust vent

Floors

  • Vacuumed and mopped, corners and along baseboards
  • Baseboards and door frames wiped by hand
  • Carpet shampoo available as an add-on

Closets & storage

  • Shelves, rods and closet floors wiped and vacuumed
  • Linen closets, pantry and laundry area
  • Inside built-ins and utility cabinets

Windows & sills

  • Windowsills and tracks cleaned out
  • Interior glass and mirrors streak-free
  • Blinds dusted, light fixtures and ceiling fans wiped

Walls, doors & details

  • Scuffs and fingerprints spot-cleaned on walls, doors and light switches
  • Outlet covers, vents and radiators dusted
  • Trash removed, all rooms left ready for keys, photos or a new tenant

Scheduling & access

We work around your closing and lease dates

Move-outs come with a hard deadline, so we plan the cleaning around it rather than the other way round. Tell us the date the home needs to be ready and we set the visit for the day before, or the morning of, so it is inspected clean.

  1. 1

    Tell us the deadline. Lease end, closing day or the listing photo date. Let us know as well if the home will already be empty. Movers out first, cleaners second, always.

  2. 2

    Keys and access. You do not need to be on site. A lockbox code, a key left with the landlord or agent, or a garage code all work. We confirm access the day before and lock up when we leave.

  3. 3

    Utilities on. We need water and power to clean. If the utilities are being shut off, ask for the switch to happen after our visit.

  4. 4

    Walk-through for larger homes. For bigger houses in Guilderland, Wilton or on the lake we do a short walk-through first so the quote and the time on site are accurate. Apartments are usually quoted from a few questions by phone or email.

All FAQs
Does the home need to be completely empty?

Empty is best. It lets us clean inside the closets, inside the cabinets and behind the appliances. If a few items have to stay, tell us when you book and we work around them.

Can you clean right after the movers leave?

Yes, when it is on the schedule. We book the cleaning for after the movers are done, usually the afternoon or the next morning. Book as early as you can once you know your dates.

What if my closing or lease date moves?

Just tell us. Rescheduling with at least 24 hours' notice is free. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice incur a 30% fee.
