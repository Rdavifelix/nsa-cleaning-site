What's included

Construction dust hides in places a regular clean never touches

Drywall and sawdust settle on top of door frames, inside cabinet boxes, in HVAC registers and on light fixtures — and it keeps falling for days. We work top to bottom, room by room.

  • Dust removal from every surface

    Ceilings, walls, trim, door frames, baseboards, window sills, shelves and countertops — vacuumed and then damp-wiped so the dust is gone, not just moved.

  • Vents, fixtures and hardware

    HVAC registers and return grilles, ceiling fans, light fixtures, outlet and switch covers, cabinet pulls, faucets and door hardware.

  • Inside cabinets, closets and drawers

    New kitchen and bathroom cabinets are cleaned inside and out, including shelves and drawers, before anything goes in. Closets and built-ins too.

  • Windows, frames and tracks

    Interior glass, frames, sills and tracks cleaned; manufacturer stickers and labels removed from windows, appliances and fixtures.

  • Paint spots, tape and adhesive

    Paint splatter, drywall mud, tape residue and adhesive on floors, glass, tile and hardware — removed carefully with the right product for each surface.

  • Floors, bathrooms and kitchen

    Vacuum, sweep and mop all floors; scrub tubs, showers, toilets and sinks; wipe down appliances inside and out; take out construction debris bags and packaging.

Phased cleaning

Rough clean, then final clean

Book one phase or both — we set it up during the walk-through with you or your contractor.

Phase 1 · Before the finish work

Rough clean

After drywall, paint and flooring are in but before fixtures and trades are done. Gets the bulk of the dust and debris out so the finish work happens in a clean space.

  • Sweep and vacuum all floors and subfloors
  • Remove debris, packaging and stickers
  • Wipe ledges, sills and window frames
  • Clean bathrooms and kitchen surfaces once

Phase 2 · Move-in ready

Final clean

After the last trade leaves. Every detail from the checklist above, so the homeowner, tenant or client walks into a finished space with no dust film and no fingerprints.

  • Full top-to-bottom dusting and damp wipe
  • Inside cabinets, closets, appliances and vents
  • Glass, mirrors, fixtures and hardware polished
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped, ready for furniture

All FAQs
When should we schedule the final clean?

After the last trade has finished and all fixtures, appliances and trim are in. If painters or flooring installers are still coming back, book the rough clean now and the final clean once they're done — otherwise the dust returns.

Do you remove construction debris?

We bag and remove packaging, small scraps, stickers and the dust and debris left behind. Large demolition waste, pallets and dumpster hauling stay with the contractor.

Can you work evenings or weekends on a commercial site?

Yes. Commercial cleaning is available 24/7, so a final clean can happen overnight before a store, office or clinic opens. Residential jobs are scheduled between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

What is your cancellation policy for construction jobs?

Schedules slip — just give us 24 hours' notice to move a visit. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice incur a 30% fee.
