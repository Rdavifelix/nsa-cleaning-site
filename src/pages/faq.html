Booking & quotes

How do I get a quote?

Send the quote form on our contact page or call {{site.business.phone}}. Tell us the service, the property type and the city, and we reply by phone or email. Quotes are free and there is no obligation.

Do you need to see the property first?

For recurring cleaning and larger jobs we like to do a short walk-through first. It is how we agree on the scope, the products we will use and any house rules. For simple jobs a phone call is often enough.

How much notice do you need to book?

The sooner the better. Send your request with a preferred date and we will confirm the earliest slot we have.

Your home

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. We bring all supplies and equipment. If you prefer that we use your own products, just let us know. Pet- and family-friendly options are available and agreed during the walk-through.

Are pets okay during a cleaning?

Yes. We prefer pets in a crate or a closed room while we work, but friendly pets are fine. We work it out with each client.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many recurring clients are at work while we clean. We agree on how we get in before the first visit, whatever you are comfortable with.

Our team

Who will be cleaning my home?

Our team is a mix of in-house staff and vetted subcontractors. Everyone is certified and trained to NSA standards, wears a gray uniform and is covered by our insurance. We speak English, Portuguese and Spanish.

Is NSA Cleaning insured?

Yes. NSA Cleaning LLC is insured. Your home and our team are covered while we work.
