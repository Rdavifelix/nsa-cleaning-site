What's included

Our turnover checklist, every single time

We work from the same checklist on every visit, whether it's a one-bedroom in Saratoga Springs or a lake house in Queensbury.

  • Linens and beds made

    We strip the beds, put on fresh sheets and make every bed hotel-style with folded towels. Keep a spare set of linens on site so the turnover never waits on laundry.

  • Bathrooms cleaned and restocked

    Toilet, shower, tub, sink, mirror and floor. We hang fresh towels and restock toilet paper, soap and shampoo from the supply closet you set up.

  • Kitchen reset

    Dishes washed or dishwasher run and emptied, counters and stovetop wiped, fridge checked for leftovers, coffee station refilled.

  • Trash and recycling out

    All bins emptied, new liners in, bags taken to the curb or the outdoor cans. Deck and fire pit area included at lake houses.

  • Floors and surfaces

    Vacuum and mop every room, dust furniture, wipe door handles, light switches and remotes, and check under beds and sofas for anything left behind.

  • Anything broken or running low, you hear about it

    If something is damaged, stained or left behind, or a supply is running out, we let you know before the next guest arrives.

All FAQs
Do you supply the linens and toiletries?

No. Hosts supply linens, towels and guest toiletries, and we restock them from your supply closet. We bring all cleaning supplies and equipment, and we tell you when stock is running low.

What if a guest checks out late or a booking changes?

Message us as soon as you know and we'll shift the visit. We ask for 24 hours' notice to cancel; cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice incur a 30% fee.

Can you turn the unit over between check-out and check-in?

Yes, when it's on the schedule. Residential crews work 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, which covers a normal check-out-to-check-in gap. Book holiday weekends early.

What happens if a guest damaged something?

We let you know right away so you can file a claim while the details are fresh. If we ever damage something ourselves, we cover it; larger items are covered by our insurance.
