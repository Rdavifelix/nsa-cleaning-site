1. What we collect

You can browse this website without telling us anything. The only place that asks you for information is the quote form. When you click a "Get a Free Quote" button, a window opens on the page with a form hosted by GoHighLevel, the software our office uses to handle new requests.

That form does not run on our server. What you type goes from your browser straight to GoHighLevel, and it is stored in our account there. We read it to prepare your quote and get back to you. What GoHighLevel does with the data on its side is covered by its own privacy policy.

The form asks for details like these:

InformationWhy it is asked
Name, email address and phone numberTo reply to you by phone or email with your quote.
Service, frequency and property typeTo understand what kind of cleaning you are asking about.
Street address and cityTo confirm you are inside our service area and plan travel.
Preferred date and messageTo schedule the walk-through or visit and note anything specific about your home or business.

The exact fields can change when we adjust the form. GoHighLevel collects and stores all of it. It may also record technical details of its own, such as your IP address and the time you sent the form, to run the form and screen out bots. We do not ask for payment details, Social Security numbers or any other sensitive information to give you a quote.

2. How we use it

We use what you send us for one purpose: to respond to your request and, if you hire us, to provide the cleaning, power washing, painting or carpet cleaning service you asked for. That includes calling or emailing you back, scheduling a walk-through, preparing your quote and service contract, and keeping a record of the job.

We do not sell your information. We do not rent it, trade it or share it with marketers. We do not add you to a newsletter or send promotional texts because you asked for a quote.

3. Who helps us process it

We are a small local company, so a few service providers handle the technical side of the website. Each one only sees what it needs to do its job.

  • Quote form. The form is hosted and run by GoHighLevel. Your request goes to GoHighLevel and is stored in our account there, where our office reads it and replies to you. GoHighLevel handles that data under its own privacy policy. Because the form sits inside a frame on our page, it can also set its own cookies or store data in your browser, and any spam protection on the form is theirs, not ours.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps short-lived server logs that can include your IP address, browser type and the pages you requested. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running. The details you type into the quote form do not pass through this server.
  • Analytics. Google Analytics 4, only if you accept the cookie banner. See the next section.

Beyond these providers, we only disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our team or property, or with your permission.

4. Cookies and analytics

This site works without cookies. The first time you visit, a small banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Nothing analytics-related loads until you choose "Accept". The quote form is loaded only when you open it, and it can store data of its own once it does.

What gets stored

ItemTypePurpose
nsa_cookie_consentBrowser local storageRemembers whether you accepted or declined analytics so the banner does not reappear on every page.
_ga, _ga_*Google Analytics cookiesSet only after you accept. They let us count visits and see which pages and cities bring people to the site. IP addresses are anonymized and advertising features are switched off (Google Consent Mode).
Storage set by the quote formCookies and browser storage from GoHighLevelThe form is hosted by GoHighLevel and loads only when you click a quote button. GoHighLevel decides what it stores there and why. Our cookie banner does not control it.

Changing your mind

Click Cookie settings in the footer of any page to reopen the banner and change your choice. If you decline, Google Analytics does not load and no _ga cookies are set. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser at any time. If you would rather not open the hosted form at all, call or email us and we will take your request that way.

Google may process analytics data on servers outside New York and the United States. You can read how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. How long we keep it

  • Quote requests stay in our GoHighLevel account, and in any email or notes that follow, as a record of the request and of any job that comes from it. We keep them as ordinary business records, for as long as we need them for scheduling, billing, insurance and tax purposes. GoHighLevel stores them for us and follows its own policy for the copies on its systems.
  • Hosting logs are kept by Vercel for a short period under its own retention policy.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics under the retention setting we choose in that tool.

7. Children

This website is meant for adults arranging cleaning services for their home or business. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us a quote request, email us and we will delete it.

8. Links to other sites

Some pages link to our Facebook page and to other websites we do not control. Once you leave nsacleaningllc.com, this policy no longer applies. Facebook and any other site you visit have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

The quote form is a similar case even though it opens on our page. It is hosted by GoHighLevel, and what you send through it is handled under GoHighLevel's privacy policy as well as this one.
