1. What we collect

The only place this website asks you for information is the quote form on our contact page and home page. You can browse every other page without telling us anything.

When you send a quote request we receive:

InformationWhy we need it
Name, email address and phone numberTo reply to you by phone or email with your quote.
Service, frequency and property typeTo understand what kind of cleaning you are asking about.
Street address and city (optional)To confirm you are inside our service area and plan travel.
Preferred date and message (optional)To schedule the walk-through or visit and note anything specific about your home or business.
The page you sent the form fromTo see which service page you were reading, so we reply with the right details.
Your IP address and the time of submissionSpam prevention only. Our form rejects bots and limits repeated submissions from the same connection.

The form also includes a hidden field that real visitors never see. Bots that fill it in are ignored. We do not ask for payment details, Social Security numbers or any other sensitive information on this website.

2. How we use it

We use what you send us for one purpose: to respond to your request and, if you hire us, to provide the cleaning, power washing, painting or carpet cleaning service you asked for. That includes calling or emailing you back, scheduling a walk-through, preparing your quote and service contract, and keeping a record of the job.

We do not sell your information. We do not rent it, trade it or share it with marketers. We do not add you to a newsletter or send promotional texts because you asked for a quote.

3. Who helps us process it

We are a small local company, so a few service providers handle the technical side of the website. Each one only sees what it needs to do its job.

  • Email delivery. When you submit the quote form, a transactional email provider (Resend or Web3Forms) delivers the message to our business inbox, {{site.business.email}}. The provider processes the form contents in order to deliver that email.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps short-lived server logs that can include your IP address, browser type and the pages you requested. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running.
  • Spam protection. When enabled, the quote form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. Turnstile may process your IP address and some browser information to make that decision. It does not show you puzzles or track you across other sites.
  • Analytics. Google Analytics 4, only if you accept the cookie banner. See the next section.

Beyond these providers, we only disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our team or property, or with your permission.

4. Cookies and analytics

This site works without cookies. The first time you visit, a small banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Nothing analytics-related loads until you choose "Accept".

What we store

ItemTypePurpose
nsa_cookie_consentBrowser local storageRemembers whether you accepted or declined analytics so the banner does not reappear on every page.
_ga, _ga_*Google Analytics cookiesSet only after you accept. They let us count visits and see which pages and cities bring people to the site. IP addresses are anonymized and advertising features are switched off (Google Consent Mode).

Changing your mind

Click Cookie settings in the footer of any page to reopen the banner and change your choice. If you decline, Google Analytics does not load and no _ga cookies are set. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser at any time.

Google may process analytics data on servers outside New York and the United States. You can read how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. How long we keep it

  • Quote emails stay in our business inbox as a record of the request and of any job that follows. We keep them as ordinary business records, for as long as we need them for scheduling, billing, insurance and tax purposes.
  • Spam-prevention data (IP address and timestamp) is only held briefly in memory while the form checks for repeated submissions, and appears in the quote email we receive.
  • Hosting logs are kept by Vercel for a short period under its own retention policy.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics under the retention setting we choose in that tool.

  • delete your quote request and related emails, unless we need to keep them for an active job, a dispute, insurance or a legal requirement.

Email {{site.business.email}} from the address you used on the form, or call {{{site.business.phoneHtml}}}. We will confirm your identity in a simple way, for example by replying to the same email address, and respond within a reasonable time. There is no charge for these requests.

7. Children

This website is meant for adults arranging cleaning services for their home or business. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us a quote request, email us and we will delete it.

8. Links to other sites

Some pages link to our Facebook page and to other websites we do not control. Once you leave nsacleaningllc.com, this policy no longer applies. Facebook and any other site you visit have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.
